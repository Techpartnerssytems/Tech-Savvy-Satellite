Section 01 // Introduction

Executive Overview

🛰️ Techsavvy Satellite — Cross-Platform RevOps Automation

Techsavvy Satellite needed to streamline its field service operations and bridge communication gaps between technicians, office staff, and customers during service dispatch calls.

Before implementing this solution, field dispatches relied on heavy manual coordination. Dispatchers copied records by hand, field technicians had to call in status changes, and clients experienced uncertainty waiting for satellite installation arrivals.

By connecting GoHighLevel, Asana, Slack, and Google Workspace through modular Make.com webhook scenarios, we transformed a fragmented manual operation into a unified, zero-friction RevOps ecosystem.

Section 02 // Operational Friction

The Challenges

Field service delivery requires precision timing and instant synchronization across systems. Techsavvy Satellite faced three core operational friction points:

📋
01

Manual Dispatch Overhead

Office managers had to manually create tasks, assign technicians, and update client records across multiple platforms.

⚠️
02

Communication Friction

Internal teams lacked real-time visibility into field technician statuses, leading to delayed project tracking and miscommunication.

⏱️
03

Poor Customer Transparency

Clients had no real-time status updates on technician arrival times, resulting in repetitive support inquiries and missed appointments.

Section 03 // RevOps Engineering

The Solution

Engineered an automated, multi-app Revenue Operations (RevOps) ecosystem connecting GoHighLevel, Asana, Slack, and Google Workspace using Make.com, REST APIs, and webhooks:

PILLAR 01 // CUSTOMER COMMUNICATIONS

Automated Customer Alerts

Configured instant SMS and email notifications to keep customers informed as technicians transition through dispatch stages (e.g., "En Route" or "On-Site").

PILLAR 02 // INTERNAL TEAM VISIBILITY

Real-Time Slack Broadcasts

Routed live dispatch activity alerts into dedicated internal Slack channels to give office teams instant field visibility.

PILLAR 03 // TASK MANAGEMENT & SYNC

Hands-Free Task Tracking

Automated task creation and status progression inside Asana as technicians updated jobs in the field.
